## Done
- **Shell.** The sidebar is now the same ivory as the canvas — no white panel, no right border, no
  edge shadow. The active item is a white pill on `--elevation-pill` with a clay icon (the left
  accent rail is gone; white-as-a-material does that job better). Inactive items are plain text
  with an outline icon and a whisper of white on hover. The mark is a dark rounded-square tile
  whose geometry and colours are now copied verbatim from P42b's `public/promoteros-mark.svg`, so
  the favicon and the rail are one drawing. A second section label (`shell.section_account`) sits
  over Settings, and the account row is wrapped in a quiet white card. The mobile top bar is ivory
  with a hairline. **The drawer's behaviour is untouched** — verified live: `role="dialog"`,
  `aria-modal="true"`, `body.overflow = hidden`, focus moved inside on open.
- **`--focus-ring-gap`.** New token. The ring is a ground-coloured gap then clay; the sidebar, the
  top bar and the account card re-point it locally so a focused control on ivory no longer wears a
  white halo. Default is white, so every existing call site is unchanged.
- **Per component.** `Card` 24px radius, warm hairline, diffuse lift; header/footer bands lost their
  tint. `Button` is a pill: near-black `primary` with no ring/gradient/inner highlight, white
  `secondary`, quiet `ghost`, sand `subtle`. `Badge` dropped its inset hairline and grew taller and
  wider — a flat pastel capsule. `StatTile` was rebuilt: label → number → delta, with the pastel
  chip moved to the **top-right corner**, the value always ink, and three new optional props
  (`delta`, `deltaTone`, `chip`). `Table` head lost its fill and kept a micro uppercase label over a
  hairline; cells gained air and `tabular-nums`. `Field` is 18px with a clay focus border (the
  accent is near-black now, so a focused field drawn in it was invisible) and a warm chevron.
  `PageHeader`/`EmptyState` icon chips are the sand pastel; `Section`'s level-2 heading dropped to
  the micro step. `Avatar`'s six tints moved off the status colours onto the pastels — a promoter is
  not "bad". `ScoreBar`'s brand fill is clay instead of an ink→clay ramp. `Skeleton` radii follow
  what they stand in for.
- **No component API changed.** Every export, prop name, variant value and default is what it was.
  The only additions are `StatTile`'s three optional props (each defaulting to today's rendering)
  and two type exports in `index.ts`. `EmptyState`'s icon chip is deliberately still a `<div>` with
  a `ring`, because `app/dashboard/page.tsx` recolours it through `[&>div:first-child]:…`.
- **Screens opened** at 1400px and at 375px: `/dashboard`, `/shifts`, `/promoters`,
  `/promoters/[id]`, `/settings`, plus `/login`, `/campaigns`, `/settings/messaging` and
  `/campaigns/[id]/report` (the only screen with a full `StatStrip` in today's seed). The mobile
  drawer was opened and probed. `documentElement.scrollWidth === clientWidth === 375` on
  `/shifts`, `/promoters` and `/settings` — nothing overflows horizontally.
- `npx tsc --noEmit` clean · `npm test` 440/440 · `scan-function-props` CLEAN ·

## Notes for the manager
- **Two values changed in `globals.css`, both with a reason.** `--shadow-sm` and `--shadow-md` lost
  most of their tight contact layer and gained a wider ambient one: at the previous weight the
  contact shadow read as a dark line under every card, which is precisely the "drop shadow" look the
  reference does not have. Everything else in the file is additive — five pastel chip triples
  (`peach`, `mint`, `lilac`, `sand`, plus `rose`, so a `bad` tone has a chip of its own rather than
  borrowing the clay family's peach), `--elevation-pill`, and `--focus-ring-gap`.
- **`app/layout.tsx` changed by one line.** `viewport.themeColor` was still `#1646B8`, the old brand
  blue; it now matches `--color-canvas`. That colour paints the Android browser chrome above the
  page, so it was the most visible remaining piece of the previous identity.
- **Buttons are `rounded-full` now.** This is the biggest single look change and it touches all 73
  screens. It is deliberate: the reference's two action shapes are a near-black pill and a white
  pill, and a pill cannot be confused with an input, a card or a table cell at any size. Inputs
  stayed at 18px rather than becoming pills, because a pill-shaped text field puts the caret in a
  curve.
- **No overlap with P42b.** It owns `public/**`, `app/login/**`, `app/privacy/**` and the token
  pages; I own the kit and the shell. The one shared surface is the mark, and I copied theirs rather
  than inventing a second version. Neither of us touched the dictionaries.
- **What I would still improve.** (1) The seed has almost no data today, so no screen in the product
  currently renders a `StatTile` `delta` — the prop is built and typed but has never been seen with
  real content, and the dashboard is where it belongs. (2) The campaign-report strip above. (3) A
  filter bar that sits directly on the canvas (`/shifts`) puts sunken-fill inputs on ivory, where
  the fill and the ground are close enough that the controls read a little muddy; inside a white
  card (`/promoters`) they are right. The honest fix is for those bars to sit in a `Card`, which is
  a page-level change in another lane. (4) Nothing in CI opens a page, so this identity is one
  careless parcel away from drifting; a screenshot check on four routes would pay for itself.
